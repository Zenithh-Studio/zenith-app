# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

Znith Studio contributors are expected to make participation respectful, constructive, and safe for people with different backgrounds and levels of experience.

## Expected behavior

- Be considerate and direct without being hostile.
- Discuss the work and its technical merits; do not attack people.
- Accept good-faith corrections and offer actionable feedback.
- Respect privacy and do not publish another person's private information.
- Keep project spaces focused on the project and its community.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, intimidation, threats, deliberate disruption, sexualized attention without consent, and publishing private information without permission are not acceptable.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately to the project maintainers through the repository host's private contact or reporting channel. Do not use a public issue for sensitive reports. Maintainers will review reports, protect reporter confidentiality where practical, and may remove content, restrict participation, or take other action appropriate to the circumstances.

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests, discussions, review comments, and other spaces where someone is representing the project.
